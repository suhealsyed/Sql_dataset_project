# Sql_dataset_project
As an aspiring data analyst, I am constantly looking for ways to bridge the gap between raw data and business value. I recently analyzed a Zepto product dataset to uncover pricing strategies and inventory trends.   
Here is a breakdown of what I executed in my latest SQL project:
Data Cleaning & Standardization: Handled missing values across critical columns, removed anomalies where the price was zero, and converted pricing metrics from paisa to rupees for accurate financial analysis.   
Inventory Gap Analysis: Identified high-value products (MRP > ₹300) that are currently out of stock, highlighting potential revenue leaks for the business.   
Revenue Estimation: Calculated the total estimated revenue for each product category based on the available quantity and discounted selling price.   
Discounting Strategy: Queried the top 5 categories offering the highest average discount percentage, while also isolating premium products (MRP > ₹500) that offer less than a 10% discount.
